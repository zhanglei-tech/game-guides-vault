Summary

Shattered Qualia is a curiosity which can be obtained during a battle against the Deepwater Guardian.

Game Description

A pattern, no more than a mere waveform, etched into an ethereal shard of glass. This fragment of data represents a trace of the electrical impulses running across a neural network. Many fragments would be required to recreate any sensible experience.

Released upon the destruction of a boundary horror.

Sources
Dropped from Child of the Waves enemies spawned by the Deepwater Guardian.
Extracted from Boundary Horrors in Expedition 16: The Cursed.
Use

Shattered Qualia is not used as an ingredient for crafting.
