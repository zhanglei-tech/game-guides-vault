~~~text
Summary

Polished Stone is a curiosity that can be harvested from ruined structures found on ruined worlds.

Game Description

The powdery remains of a highly polished mineral surface. It glitters as it catches the light, like fiery grains of sand.

Acquired by applying the Mining Laser to the structural remnants located on ruined worlds.

Source

An example of a Ruined Structure.

Can be harvested from Ruined Structure found on ruined worlds using a Mining Beam. These structures are exclusive to the Reliquary planetary biome, and its 22 name variants.

Small amounts can also be gained from defeating Vigilant Stone guardians.

Use

Upgrades

Polished Stone is used to upgrade buildings in your Planetary Settlement.

Crafting

Polished Stone is not used as an ingredient for crafting.

Refining

Polished Stone is used as an ingredient for refining the following products using a Refiner:

 Pure Ferrite  --  Polished Stone x1  →   Pure Ferrite x2  ("Recycle Stone", 0.12 sec./unit output)
Cooking

Polished Stone is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.

Additional Information

The Ruined planets come in the following names:

LUSH RUINED - Abandoned Paradise,   Overgrown,   Overgrown Relic,   The Reliquary
BARREN RUINED - Abandoned,   Ruined,   Ruined Dustbowl,   Abandoned Desert
SCORCHED RUINED - Ruined,   Scorched Relic,   Lost to Fire,   Abandoned Crucible
FROZEN RUINED - Abandoned,   Icy Ruin  , Frozen Relic
TOXIC RUINED - Abandoned,   Bleached Ruin,   Decaying Ruin
RADIOACTIVE RUINED - Ruined,   Claimed By Decay,   Mutating Relic
DEAD RUINED - Abandoned
~~~
